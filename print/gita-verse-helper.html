Gita Shloka Recitation · Judges' verse helper
Classes 1 to 4 · group A
These are only our suggestions — you may recite ANY verses from the Bhagavad-gita you already know.

Class 1 to 2Learn any ONE verse

BG 18.65
man-mana bhava mad-bhakto
mad-yaji mam namaskuru
mam evaisyasi satyam te
pratijane priyo 'si me
Think of Me, worship Me, bow to Me — and you will come to Me.
BG 9.26
patram puspam phalam toyam
yo me bhaktya prayacchati
tad aham bhakty-upahrtam
asnami prayatatmanah
A leaf, a flower, a fruit, water — offered with love, I accept it.
BG 9.27
yat karosi yad asnasi
yaj juhosi dadasi yat
yat tapasyasi kaunteya
tat kurusva mad-arpanam
Whatever you do, eat, offer or give — do it for Me.
BG 1.1
dhrtarastra uvaca
dharma-ksetre kuru-ksetre
samaveta yuyutsavah
mamakah pandavas caiva
kim akurvata sanjaya
The opening verse of the Gita — Dhritarashtra asks what happened on the field of Kurukshetra.
The 'dhrtarastra uvaca' line may be left out by the youngest reciters.
BG 10.12-13
param brahma param dhama
pavitram paramam bhavan
purusam sasvatam divyam
adi-devam ajam vibhum
Arjuna addresses Krishna as the supreme truth, the supreme abode, the purest, eternal and unborn.
Vedabase shows this as the combined verse 10.12-13, so the page is longer than the lines above.

Class 3 to 4Learn any TWO verses

BG 4.7
yada yada hi dharmasya
glanir bhavati bharata
abhyutthanam adharmasya
tadatmanam srjamy aham
Whenever dharma declines and adharma rises, I appear.
Best learnt together with 4.8 — the two are one thought.
BG 4.8
paritranaya sadhunam
vinasaya ca duskrtam
dharma-samsthapanarthaya
sambhavami yuge yuge
To protect the good and re-establish dharma, I appear in every age.
BG 5.29
bhoktaram yajna-tapasam
sarva-loka-mahesvaram
suhrdam sarva-bhutanam
jnatva mam santim rcchati
Knowing Me as the friend of every living being, one attains peace.
Many students already know this one — it is chanted before meals.
BG 18.66
sarva-dharman parityajya
mam ekam saranam vraja
aham tvam sarva-papebhyo
moksayisyami ma sucah
Give up all else and simply surrender to Me. Do not fear.
BG 2.47
karmany evadhikaras te
ma phalesu kadacana
ma karma-phala-hetur bhur
ma te sango 'stv akarmani
You have a right to your work, but never to its results.

How each recitation is scored — 10 marks each, 50 total

  1. Pronunciation (Uchcharana) — are the Sanskrit sounds clear and correct?
  2. Memory & Accuracy — recited from memory, without prompting or skipped lines.
  3. Tone & Melody (Svara) — steady rhythm and pleasing intonation.
  4. Confidence & Presentation — posture, voice reaching the room, composure.
  5. Overall Impression — the feeling and sincerity the child brings.
A student may recite any verse from the Bhagavad-gita, including one not printed here. Judge an unlisted verse exactly as you would a listed one — never mark a child down for choosing their own. Younger reciters may leave out an opening speaker line such as dhrtarastra uvaca.

UTKARSH 2026
Gita Shloka Recitation · Judges' verse helper
Classes 5 to 10 · groups B and C
These are only our suggestions — you may recite ANY verses from the Bhagavad-gita you already know.

Class 5 to 7Learn any THREE verses

BG 2.13
dehino 'smin yatha dehe
kaumaram yauvanam jara
tatha dehantara-praptir
dhiras tatra na muhyati
As the body passes from childhood to youth to age, so the soul passes to another body. The wise are not confused by this.
BG 7.7
mattah parataram nanyat
kincid asti dhananjaya
mayi sarvam idam protam
sutre mani-gana iva
Everything rests upon Me, as pearls are strung on a thread.
BG 9.22
ananyas cintayanto mam
ye janah paryupasate
tesam nityabhiyuktanam
yoga-ksemam vahamy aham
For those who worship Me with undivided attention, I carry what they lack and preserve what they have.
BG 3.21
yad yad acarati sresthas
tat tad evetaro janah
sa yat pramanam kurute
lokas tad anuvartate
Whatever a great person does, others follow.
BG 2.14
matra-sparsas tu kaunteya
sitosna-sukha-duhkha-dah
agamapayino 'nityas
tams titiksasva bharata
Happiness and distress come and go like winter and summer. Learn to tolerate them.
BG 2.27
jatasya hi dhruvo mrtyur
dhruvam janma mrtasya ca
tasmad apariharye 'rthe
na tvam socitum arhasi
For one who is born, death is certain. Do not grieve over what cannot be avoided.
BG 4.34
tad viddhi pranipatena
pariprasnena sevaya
upadeksyanti te jnanam
jnaninas tattva-darsinah
Approach a teacher humbly, ask questions, and render service.

Class 8 to 10Learn any FOUR verses

BG 2.62
dhyayato visayan pumsah
sangas tesupajayate
sangat sanjayate kamah
kamat krodho 'bhijayate
Dwelling on the senses breeds attachment; attachment breeds desire; desire breeds anger.
Recite together with 2.63 as one chain. The two count as two verses.
BG 2.63
krodhad bhavati sammohah
sammohat smrti-vibhramah
smrti-bhramsad buddhi-naso
buddhi-nasat pranasyati
From anger comes delusion, then loss of memory, then loss of intelligence — and one falls.
BG 2.20
na jayate mriyate va kadacin
nayam bhutva bhavita va na bhuyah
ajo nityah sasvato 'yam purano
na hanyate hanyamane sarire
The soul is never born and never dies. It is not slain when the body is slain.
Longer metre than the others — this one tests breath and pacing.
BG 12.13-14
advesta sarva-bhutanam
maitrah karuna eva ca
nirmamo nirahankarah
sama-duhkha-sukhah ksami

santustah satatam yogi
yatatma drdha-niscayah
mayy arpita-mano-buddhir
yo mad-bhaktah sa me priyah
The qualities of a devotee — friendly to all, free of ego, equal in joy and sorrow, and very dear to Krishna.
Vedabase shows these as one combined verse. Counts as two.
BG 6.5
uddhared atmanatmanam
natmanam avasadayet
atmaiva hy atmano bandhur
atmaiva ripur atmanah
Lift yourself by your own mind. The mind is your friend, and also your enemy.
BG 15.15
sarvasya caham hrdi sannivisto
mattah smrtir jnanam apohanam ca
vedais ca sarvair aham eva vedyo
vedanta-krd veda-vid eva caham
I am seated in everyone's heart; from Me come memory, knowledge and forgetfulness.
BG 16.1-3
abhayam sattva-samsuddhir
jnana-yoga-vyavasthitih
danam damas ca yajnas ca
svadhyayas tapa arjavam

(continues through 16.3)
The divine qualities — fearlessness, purity, charity, self-control, study, austerity and simplicity.
Three verses in one, so this nearly fills the requirement by itself.
